My name is Hem Raj Dhakal. I am a first-generation serial entrepreneur from Baglung, Nepal, and the Co-Founder and Managing Director of IME Group — one of Nepal's largest and most diversified business conglomerates. Since co-founding IME alongside my brother Chandra Prasad Dhakal, my focus has been on building institutions that genuinely improve lives.

IME began with a clear purpose: to help the millions of Nepali migrant workers in the Middle East and Malaysia send money home safely, affordably, and quickly. That mission transformed into a group that today operates across banking, remittance, digital finance, trading, IT, communications, energy, tourism, automobiles, insurance, and infotainment — with over 45,000 jobs created in total.

Over more than two decades, I have lived and built businesses in Japan, Malaysia, the United Kingdom, and the United States, establishing financial service institutions across South and Southeast Asia. I led several mergers and acquisitions, including partnerships with companies listed on NASDAQ. In 2009, we co-founded the IME Foundation to invest in education, scholarships, and the next generation of Nepali entrepreneurs.

My philosophy is straightforward: a team that owns its work builds something lasting. The driving force of everything we do at IME is our slogan — Pioneering Spirit. You can learn more about the group at imegroup.com.np.
